## 2026-11-08

- Fixed rulers starting far above their domain limit with almost no levies (new Historical Demesne rule, on by default).
- New Mortal Rabble Size rule: keep PoD's small levies or get vanilla-sized armies.
- Grand cities (Paris, Rome, Constantinople...) now give taxes and levies.
- World of Secrets / World of Corruption: counties start with their vanilla faiths.
- Fixed Rome being cursed by "Spew Wide the Pit" at game start.

## 2026-11-07

- Immortals (vampires, mummies, fae...) now look their proper age on vanilla bookmarks.
- Compatibility with Divine Intervention Cheat Menu (PoD interaction categories no longer wiped).
- Fixed a crash with characters without culture or faith.
